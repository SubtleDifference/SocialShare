A little easier to share.

Small, accessible sharing controls. No icon fonts, frameworks or external requests until you choose a destination.

Drop it into a page

Choose the destinations you need. The library builds labelled links and buttons.

<nav class="socialshare inlinesocials"
     aria-label="Share this page"
     data-ss-platforms="facebook linkedin bluesky email copy"></nav>

Make it your own

Use CSS variables for the colour, shape and spacing. Share opens the device chooser where available; otherwise it becomes Copy link.

.socialshare.customtheme {
    --ss-background: #4e1741;
    --ss-radius: 8px;
    --ss-gap: 12px;
}

Your existing markup still works

Keep the layout classes, service classes and data attributes. Extra styling classes are welcome. Your own icons or inline SVGs can stay inside each control.

  • Facebook
  • X / Twitter
  • Copy link

Or keep sharing within reach

The fixed group sits at the left on larger screens and at the bottom on smaller screens. Keep the list short and leave space for it in your page layout.
